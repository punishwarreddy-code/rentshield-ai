AI Investigation Explained

How RentShield checks your listing

We evaluate 5 distinct risk pillars, cross-reference local market pricing, and adapt our depth based on the patterns discovered.

1

Price Deviation Analysis

RentShield compares the stated monthly rent against the median rental baseline for that specific pin code or neighborhood. When a luxury apartment is offered at 50% or more below market price, it automatically raises a critical price alert.

2

Perceptual Image Fingerprinting

Scammers steal images from architectural firms, luxury hotels, and overseas rental portfolios. We calculate a perceptual visual hash of the listing images to check if they have appeared previously on unrelated websites.

3

Description Semantics & Storytelling Traps

Scammers rely on recurring emotional scripts: "transfer abroad", "cannot show the house today because key is with courier", or "military emergency". Our text classifier identifies manipulative storytelling patterns.

4

Payment & Deposit Urgency Checks

The single biggest red flag is asking for money before the tenant physically steps inside the flat. We specifically inspect whether tokens, gate-pass fees, or security deposits are demanded up-front.

5

Location & Geometry Coherence

Checks whether the listed address, area landmarks, and stated building specifications match authentic municipal zoning and geographic coordinates.
